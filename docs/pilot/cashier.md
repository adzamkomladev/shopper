# Cashier guide

For the person on the register during the pilot.

## Start of day

1. Sign in with your email and password. The register remembers a sign-in for 7 days, so you can sign in
   offline if you signed in on this register this week.
2. Open your shift: count the float and enter it. Checkout stays closed until the shift is open.

## Selling

- Scan, or type a name, code or barcode in the search box (F2 gets you there). Enter adds the item.
- Change a quantity with F4, remove a line with Delete, hold a sale with F6 and bring it back with F7.
- Ctrl+Enter goes to payment. Type the cash handed over, or pick a quick amount, then F5 to charge.
  Alt+2 is mobile money, Alt+3 card, Alt+4 splits the payment.
- The receipt prints by itself. Enter starts the next sale.

## Help on the register

- F1 (or Ctrl+/, or the ? button) lists every shortcut, with a search box.
- The sidebar on the left opens with Ctrl+M: Previous purchases, Shift, Cash drop, No sale, Account
  payment and Register settings.
- The dot in the top right says whether the register reaches the server. Offline you can keep selling;
  sales send themselves when the connection is back.

## When something is wrong

- "Your session ended. Sign in again." means your sign-in expired (they last a working day): sign in again
  and carry on.
- "Sign-in is locked" after too many wrong passwords: a manager can unlock it from the sign-in screen.
- A price that looks wrong, a refund or a void needs a manager's PIN. Do not guess; call them.
- If the register will not start or print, tell the manager. Do not restart the PC mid-sale.

## End of day

Open Shift (Alt+H), count the drawer and close the shift. Hand the count to the manager.
