# Shopper 1.0.0

18 November 2026. The first full release of Shopper, the point of sale for Ghanaian supermarkets. One product, two
ways to run it: a cloud subscription, or a sealed box in the shop that needs no internet.

## What you get

**At the till**

- Sells with or without internet. Sales made offline send themselves when the connection is back.
- Barcode scanning (including weighed and priced labels), receipt printing and the cash drawer.
- Cash, mobile money, card and split payments; holds, voids, refunds and price changes behind a manager's PIN.
- Shifts with a counted float, cash drops and a Z-report at close. Every action has a keyboard shortcut.

**In the back office**

- A setup wizard: store, branches, tax, products (from a spreadsheet), team and tills.
- Products, prices, scale labels, stock receiving, counts and valuation, suppliers.
- Customers with credit, deposits, statements and loyalty.
- Tax by date, the VAT return, and GRA E-VAT submission for every sale.
- Reports, the general ledger every report reads from, and an audit log that proves nothing was changed.
- Staff roles that decide exactly what each person can do.

**Online**

- An online store for each shop: customers order and pay, the shop picks and hands over.

**On your own box**

- A box on the shop's network runs everything with no internet: the registers find it by themselves.
- Nightly checked backups, signed updates by USB that undo themselves if they fail, and a spare box restored from
  the last backup.

**Your data is yours**

- A Shopper Archive of the whole business at any time, and moving between the cloud and a box in either direction
  with nothing lost.

## Updating

- **Registers** update themselves when they next close, never during a shift. 1.0.0 goes to the pilot shop first and
  to every shop once it has run there.
- **Cloud** shops have nothing to do.
- **Boxes** install the 1.0.0 bundle from Billing → Updates.

## Known limits

- A move between the cloud and a box cannot be scheduled for later, and its time estimate is a rough guide until a
  real move has been measured.
- The box keeps receipts and archives on its own disk; the S3-style file store is not in the installer yet.
- The box has been proven in test machines, not yet on shop hardware.
- Card and mobile money terminals, scales over a serial cable, promotions, purchase orders and transfers between
  branches are not in 1.0.0. They are next.
