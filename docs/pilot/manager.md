# Manager guide

For the shop manager and owner during the pilot. The back office is at https://shopper.temtem.dev (and on
the register under Back office, when it is online).

## Daily

- **Reports** (Overview) shows today's sales, top products and tenders. Check it at close.
- **Sales > Transactions** finds any sale by receipt number, customer or amount; refunds start here.
- **Sales > Reconciliation** compares each closed shift's counted cash with what it should be.
- **Inventory > Stock** shows what is low. Receive a delivery under Inventory > Receive.

## Staff and access

- **Organisation > Users** invites staff and sets their role. Roles decide what each person can do on the
  register; cashiers cannot void, refund or change prices without a manager's PIN.
- A cashier locked out after wrong passwords: unlock them on the register's sign-in screen with your PIN.

## Getting around

- The sidebar groups every page; Ctrl+B collapses it to icons. The search button (or `/`, or Ctrl+K)
  jumps to any page or product. `?` lists the keyboard shortcuts.
- Your name at the bottom of the sidebar has your account, the theme (light, dark or system) and Sign out.

## When something is wrong

- A register that shows "Offline" keeps selling and catches up by itself. If it stays offline for more
  than an hour while the shop has internet, tell support.
- Anything that looks wrong in the numbers: note the receipt number and time and tell support. Do not
  edit stock to make a report match.
- Support during the pilot: the Shopper team on WhatsApp. Say the shop code, register name and what you
  saw.
